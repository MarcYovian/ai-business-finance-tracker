# Privacy Policy for AI-Fi Tracker

**Last Updated: October 10, 2025**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to AI-Fi Tracker ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our SaaS application, AI-Fi Tracker (the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

### a. Personal Information

-   **Email Address**: When you register for an account, we collect your email address. This is used for authentication and communication purposes, managed through our authentication provider, Supabase Auth.

### b. Financial Data

-   As a core function of the Service, we collect and store the financial data you provide. This includes, but is not limited to, transaction descriptions, amounts, dates, categories, fund source names, and account balances.

### c. Third-Party API Keys

-   **"Bring Your Own Key" (BYOK) Model**: Our AI Assistant feature requires you to provide your own API key for a third-party AI service (such as OpenAI or Google Gemini).
-   **Local Storage**: These API keys are stored **exclusively in your browser's `localStorage` on your own device**. We **never** transmit these keys to our servers, and we do not have access to them.

### d. Usage Data

-   We may collect general, non-personal information about how the Service is accessed and used ("Usage Data"). This Usage Data may include information such as your computer's Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, browser version, the pages of our Service that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages, and other diagnostic data. This helps us understand user behavior and improve the Service.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for various purposes:

-   To provide, maintain, and improve our Service.
-   To authenticate you and manage your account.
-   To process, store, and display your financial data as you direct.
-   To respond to your requests and provide customer support.
-   To send you important account-related communications, such as service announcements or security alerts.

## 4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We only share your information with third-party services as necessary to provide our Service.

### a. Supabase

-   All of your account information (email address) and financial data are stored and managed using Supabase, a backend-as-a-service platform that provides database and authentication services. Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database managed by Supabase.

### b. AI Providers (OpenAI & Google Gemini)

-   **AI Assistant Feature**: When you use the AI Assistant, the textual input you provide (e.g., "I spent $20 on coffee") is sent to the third-party AI service (OpenAI or Google Gemini) you have configured.
-   **Use of Your Own API Key**: This data is processed by the AI provider using **your personal API key**, which you provide in the application's settings.
-   **Third-Party Privacy Policies**: The handling of your data by these AI providers is governed by their respective privacy policies. We strongly advise you to review the privacy policies of [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy) and [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
-   **Disclaimer**: AI-Fi Tracker is not responsible for the data privacy or security practices of third-party AI providers when you use your own API key.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

-   We rely on the security measures implemented by our trusted cloud provider, Supabase, to protect your data.
-   As stated above, your sensitive third-party API keys (for OpenAI/Gemini) are **not stored on our servers**. They are stored on your local device, which gives you full control over them.
-   While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## 6. User Rights and Data Control

You have control over your data within AI-Fi Tracker.

-   **Access and Update**: You can access and update your financial data at any time through your account dashboard.
-   **Data Deletion**: You can delete individual transactions, categories, or fund sources within the application.
-   **Account Deletion**: If you wish to delete your entire account and all associated data, please contact us at the email address provided below. Upon your request, we will permanently delete your account and all associated financial records from our database.

## 7. Data Retention

We will retain your Personal Information and Financial Data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Service. If you delete your account, we will delete your information, as described in the section above.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with Personal Information, please contact us.
